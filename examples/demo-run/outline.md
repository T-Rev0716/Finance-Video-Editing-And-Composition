## What it means for you

### Mortgage

- The average 30-year fixed mortgage rate was 7.11% as of Nov 12, 2026. (F1)
- Worked example: a 30-year loan for 80% of the median home price ($393,300 in Q3 2026, so a $314,640 loan) costs about $2,117 a month in principal and interest at 7.11%. That is about $185 a month more than at the rate a year ago (6.22%). (F16)
- If you already have a fixed-rate mortgage, your payment does not change. This matters if you are buying, refinancing or have an adjustable rate.

### Job

- The unemployment rate was 4.1% as of October 2026. (F17)
- That is down 0.3 percentage points from September 2025, when it was 4.4% (the closest reading to a year earlier; October 2025 was never published). (F18)
- Explain what this means for someone job-hunting or asking for a raise: a tighter market gives workers more leverage, a looser one gives employers more.

### Groceries

- Grocery prices (CPI food at home) rose 1.8% over the 12 months ending September 2026. (F20)
- That is slower than the 2.1% pace in August 2026. (F21)
- This is the change in average grocery prices across the country. Your own basket can be higher or lower.

### 401(k)

- The S&P 500 was 7,417 as of Nov 16, 2026. (F23)
- That is up 11.2% from a year earlier, when it was 6,672 (Nov 17, 2025). (F24)
- Most 401(k) stock funds follow the S&P 500. This is a snapshot of where it stands, not a prediction and not a reason to change anything.
